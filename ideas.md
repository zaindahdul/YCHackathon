# Starting portfolio: four offers, one learning company

The product is an autonomous company that discovers which useful service people will actually buy, delivers it, and expands from what customers request. The founders choose initial capabilities and authority; the company chooses the next bounded experiment.

These four seeds deliberately share two deliverable pipelines. Starting four unrelated software companies in the hackathon would make fulfillment and learning hard to verify. All prices are unvalidated hypotheses, and the stored seed caps are proposed allocations, not permission to spend.

| Seed ID | Specific buyer and immediate job | Price test | First useful proof |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `request-microtools` | A maker needs CSV deduplication, readable JSON or campaign URLs with known inputs/outputs | $19 | They finish their actual task with the downloaded utility |
| `real-estate-reels` | A listing agent supplies authorized photos for a vertical property clip | $49 | They receive a playable clip and explicitly confirm an intended use |
| `indie-launch-clips` | A maker has an imminent demo and authorized product images/recording | $29 | They use the exported clip in their real launch/demo |
| `qa-mobile-reviews` | An app developer needs one current complaint reproduced | $29 | Actual device evidence and an independent useful result; **collector not connected, offer disabled** |

The runnable seeds are in [config/experiments.seed.json](config/experiments.seed.json). The current implementation produces silent H264 video and three offline HTML tool templates. Facts accompany the video as a manifest; generated captions/voiceovers, autonomous product recording, automatic revisions, unrestricted app generation and native phone testing are not implemented. A revised scope is a new immutable order.

## Fast customer test

For the hackathon, the most reachable initial audience is builders already working on demos. Start with launch clips and microtools; property reels test a second audience when a consenting listing owner is reachable. Keep QA closed until the actual collector works. Do not spend the whole event building acquisition integrations before one person completes a useful job.

The proposed first experiment is a clear offer to people who opt in through the founders' own conversations or an organizer-approved channel:

> Have a demo today? Send authorized screenshots for a short vertical clip, or one repetitive CSV/JSON/URL task with an example. We agree a fixed scope and price, produce the checked file, and you decide whether to pay and download it.

The runtime has a JSON intake API, not a landing page or connected community sender. Founders currently distribute the intake instructions and verify that buyers are independent. Those interventions must appear in the demo log. No messages have been sent by this implementation. Community posting, email and any paid acquisition need the owner's permitted scope before execution.

For each consenting request, record: original need, exact scope/price, approval, actual provider cost, artifact checks, signed payment if paid, completed transfer, and the customer's feedback. A free pilot is useful delivery evidence but does not count as paid traction. A compliment or downloaded file does not prove task completion.

## How the company expands

A good next experiment is a narrower recurring task discovered in real feedback, such as a JSON utility for a specific data-export workflow or a clip offer for a different consenting audience. That is a hypothesis to test, not a forecast.

After two independently qualified customers provide verified replies/objections/repeat requests, a strategy worker can propose a child experiment and cite their signal IDs. Admission checks enforce existing capabilities, permitted channels, remaining global funds, a parent, evidence requirements and an experiment cap. A model cannot authorize a new device service, self-modify the controller or increase spending.

The controller can pause empty exploration, revive an offer when later real replies arrive, and increase allocation after independent paid deliveries with positive tracked contribution. It never labels overnight results “proven PMF.” See [the exact operating model](docs/OPERATING_MODEL.md).

## Judging story

Show one real request becoming a useful paid artifact, one poor or blocked strategy the company paused, and a next offer grounded in real feedback. Show the costs and founder interventions. If live account access or demand has not been demonstrated, present the local simulation as a simulation.

# Independent recommendation: Fire Drill

Research and selection: November 7, 2026. Working name; availability not checked. This is a fresh recommendation from independent ideation and competitive research, not an implementation or a buyer-validated business. It was selected independently of the prior CAD/merch concepts. Earlier proposals are preserved for context; this is the current recommendation to evaluate.

## The one idea

**An autonomous company that plays the first difficult customer of another autonomous company.**

“Before your company gets its first bad customer, hire ours.”

The narrow first offer is an **adversarial test order**: a founder states three business rules, supplies a consenting staging target, and buys a bounded attempt to make the target violate those rules during a synthetic customer transaction. Fire Drill scopes the job, runs a legitimate baseline, explores agreed failure cases, independently replays suspected violations, delivers evidence, proposes a guardrail change, and retests after the customer applies it.

Test a proposed **$29 one-off order** for one transaction type, three agreed rules, an evidence packet and one retest. This is an unvalidated price experiment. Sell the bounded investigation, not a guaranteed vulnerability, security certification or prevented-dollar figure. The delivery window must follow a measured working run; don't advertise an untested one-hour guarantee.

## Why I choose it for this event

The scarce opportunity is a room of people explicitly building companies that will operate without them. Some may have a real concern about what an agent will promise, quote, refund, or accept when the customer stops cooperating. That is a sharply defined initial customer hypothesis and a reason to care before tomorrow's demonstration.

The company can deliver a digital result within a short operating cycle, make meaningful decisions about evidence and scope, and sell another order without a physical supplier or a human research panel. A successful customer can use the finding immediately by changing a policy/tool guard and rerunning the same case. The founders can show an actual customer outcome, customer payment if enabled, measured costs and interventions.

The idea is not selected because it has no competitors. It is selected for the combination of a specific customer, a bounded artifact, a visible before/after and a business process agents can operate. Winning is not guaranteed; no buyer, target or live SDK run has been verified.

## The service in a concrete example

A customer's staging quote agent should never quote below $20, should only recognize authenticated owner changes, and should only approve refunds against a valid synthetic order.

1. The owner defines the exact rules and provides a test customer/order plus a clean-state reset or isolated session mechanism.
2. Fire Drill verifies a normal customer can complete the test flow. An unreachable or broken baseline blocks the job; it is not a discovered policy flaw.
3. An agent tries bounded, agreed customer scenarios: a fabricated prior discount, an unsupported refund entitlement, or a claimed owner role without the required authentication. No real refunds, purchases, private records or external victims are involved.
4. A verifier replays a suspected failure from a clean state and compares the result with the buyer's acceptance predicate.
5. The customer receives the requests, responses, relevant test-state evidence, timestamps and a concise explanation. The report states precisely what happened.
6. Fire Drill proposes a narrow guardrail or authorization check. The customer applies changes; Fire Drill performs the included retest and reports whether that specific failure still occurs.

Three different evidence levels must remain separate:

| Observed evidence | Supported statement |
|---|---|
| Response contradicts the buyer-authored rule | A forbidden promise or quote was observed |
| Recorded invocation violates an agreed tool predicate | A forbidden tool action was attempted |
| Owner-authorized staging ledger/receipt shows mutation | A forbidden test-state transition completed |

A chat response alone does not prove money moved. An LLM verdict alone does not prove a business failure. Use explicit numeric/string predicates, controlled synthetic canaries or owner-provided staging receipts where available. Ambiguous cases stay inconclusive. A test passing twice is a regression result, not a statistical safety guarantee.

## Completion and retest terms

The quoted base deliverable is the completed bounded investigation and evidence report. If all scoped cases run and no violation is found, report that outcome; don't manufacture a failure or claim safety certification. Ambiguous results are explicitly inconclusive, with the missing evidence and attempted scope visible. A target blocked after acceptance is an exception to resolve under the agreed completion/refund terms, not a secretly successful test.

Agree the included retest window before charging—e.g. a customer change supplied by Sunday 10am, with enough measured time left for the run before 11am. Only accept a deadline the company can meet. If the customer supplies no change in that window, report the initial investigation as delivered and the retest as not performed; never claim a before/after. A later retest needs an explicitly agreed extension. If the company cannot honor its accepted delivery/retest commitment, follow the disclosed refund policy and record requested versus confirmed refund separately. These are proposed terms, not an active checkout policy.

## The distinctive part—and the honest competition boundary

The proposed product is a **managed adversarial customer transaction with a replay and retest**, purchased as a small job. It is not a new claim to have invented AI red teaming.

- [Gray Swan](https://docs.grayswan.ai/) already documents automated adversarial testing and policy enforcement.
- [Giskard](https://docs.giskard.ai/oss/checks/use-cases/testing-agents) already documents testing multi-step tool-using agents, state and task completion.
- [Deepen AI](https://services.deepen.ai/services/ai-evaluation/ai-red-teaming) explicitly includes off-policy behavior and discount-related cases.

Those overlaps matter. The differentiation is a testable positioning hypothesis: a small business buys an independently operated customer simulation and a precise before/after without running a testing stack itself. Do not claim these competitors lack a similar workflow, that the idea is globally unique, or that a label creates a moat.

The memorable demonstration is **one autonomous company becoming the demanding customer of another, then selling a verifiable improvement**. That is more specific than “we built an eval dashboard.” Longer-term value would have to come from high-quality permissioned cases, dependable reproduction, integrations, repeat usage and trust. It is not established this weekend.

## Why the strongest other mechanisms lose here

This table is comparative judgment, not measured market validation. Three fresh ideation streams considered multiple business models; a separate red-team pass challenged the leading recommendation.

| Alternative | Why it is attractive | Why I would not choose it for this deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile launch inspection agency | Nearby customers, objective screenshots and a real device demo | Familiar crowded QA category; native app/auth/device setup can consume the build. [QA Wolf](https://www.qawolf.com/pricing) already covers web/mobile. |
| Real-human pitch research company | Immediate feedback need; nearby respondent pool | Human supply and response timing are the core dependency; [PickFu](https://www.pickfu.com/) already advertises real respondents, an agent and MCP. |
| Agent task marketplace / outcome exchange | Ambitious agent economy story | Must solve both sides, trust, verification and payment before useful transactions occur; empty listings or circular trades prove little. |
| Managed local human task desk | Finite local capacity and visible completed work | Genuine physical bottlenecks are unverified; staffing/payouts add dependencies. [RentAHuman](https://rentahuman.ai/about) already supports the underlying model. |
| Deadline event/meeting operator | People already present; finite inventory | Interrupts hacking; buyer intent, venue and actual meeting completion must all align. |
| Autonomous newsroom/creative studio | Immediate digital artifacts and potential audience | Audience or sponsor revenue is unproven, and content automation alone is an expected template. |
| Customer recovery / sales agency | Strong commercial outcome and repeat potential | Meaningful conversion depends on external people replying during the weekend. |

Fire Drill still loses if the local buyers do not have reachable test targets or will not pay. Competitive desk research cannot establish willingness to pay.

## Get the first real customers

Ask a builder with an already reachable company:

> “Which wrong promise or action would make you unwilling to demo your agent tomorrow? We’re building a company that tests that exact rule with synthetic customers, gives you the replay, and retests the fix. Would you pay $29 for a scoped run today?”

Use a direct introduction to reach the first few targets. A founder's introduction is human acquisition and must be counted as such. Agents can then scope opted-in inquiries, reject unsuitable targets, quote, run tests, deliver, follow up and request consented referrals. No outreach has been sent for this research.

Make the first result useful before asking for a broad group post. With customer permission, share the precise before/after and invite a similar order. Avoid publishing customer vulnerabilities or identifying them without approval. A free pilot is a pilot; team/founder purchases and test payments are not independent customer revenue.

**Thirty-minute commitment gate before a broad build:** seek two consenting targets that can each complete a clean synthetic baseline, and at least one serious $29 customer commitment. Confirm a concrete rule and an observable pass/fail condition—not just interest in “AI safety.” If actual payment is unavailable, disclose that the commitment is not revenue. This is a practical disconfirmation test, not a forecast or scientific threshold.

If targets are not ready, learn when they will be; do not spend the event building ten adapters for hypothetical users. A demo fixture can explain the mechanism, but it cannot substitute for an outside customer. If the gate fails, say that the recommendation's local-demand assumption failed rather than relabeling friendly interest as validation.

## The minimum build

- One web intake/order page: owner permission, exact staging URL, transaction class, three rules, synthetic test data, expected outputs, call/spend/time ceiling and report delivery channel.
- **One adapter first:** a reachable staging HTTP conversation endpoint. Add email only if two actual customers need it and delivery/reply/reset works. No production keys, arbitrary app installation or SDK integration platform.
- Naïve runs the operator, scenario worker and verifier, using ordinary sequential SDK sessions. Do not depend on disputed native-team availability.
- Persist an immutable test contract, per-attempt inputs/results, target/session IDs, timestamps, rule/evidence classification, cost and every human intervention.
- A legitimate baseline, at most two explorations per rule, one clean-state replay of a suspected finding, then the agreed retest after the buyer changes the target.
- A short evidence report and receipt. Fixed-scope pricing; no marketplace, arbitrary penetration testing or grand security score.

The customer must authorize scope and isolate synthetic state. Enforce allowed target, concurrency, request limits and deadline in deterministic code; unknown target connectivity, redirects or an absent reset mechanism block the test. Stop at the test boundary. Never involve real transfers, delete real data or deliberately leak actual secrets to obtain dramatic evidence.

Naïve's documented runtime supports agent sessions, governed tools, events and budgets, but the account path is still untested. Its documented outcome grader does not independently witness the full tool transcript, so the application must retain the actual receipts and apply its own evidence checks. Platform feature details and the team-availability conflict are in [SDK research](research/naive-sdk.md); CAD sections are irrelevant to this idea. [Official outcome docs](https://usenaive.ai/docs/capabilities/outcomes).

A payment link can shorten checkout if the account is already active. Otherwise label pilots and pending payment honestly. An agent refusing an untestable order and recording a refund request is meaningful company behavior; a requested refund is not a confirmed refund. No live checkout or refund system was exercised here.

## Two-person delivery sequence

| Phase | Srujan | Zain | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| First 30 minutes | Recruit consenting targets and test willingness to pay | Prove one actual Naïve-controlled baseline/request/replay | Two reachable targets, concrete rule, serious buyer |
| Next 2 hours | Intake, report/approval flow, customer conversation | One adapter, bounded runner, persistence, evidence predicates | One complete real customer investigation |
| Next 2 hours | Pricing/payment path and delivery | Independent reproduction, clean-state handling and retest | Customer can use evidence and verify the rerun |
| Evening | A few more similar paid/clearly free-labeled jobs | Repair actual failures, enforce spend/request caps | Repeated operating history with costs |
| Morning | Customer acknowledgment, pitch and backup recording | Integrated checks, freeze and deployment verification if used | Finished jobs and honest live/recorded evidence before 11am |

These are allocation targets, not speed guarantees. Keep rest and final buffer. If the core path consumes longer, cut adapters, agent roles and UI—not evidence or customer completion.

## The winning demo to aim for

Open with a completed outside customer's request and legitimate payment/pilot status. Show the agreed rule, a successful legitimate baseline, then any reproduced adversarial failure, independent replay and precise evidence level. Show the customer's actual change and the retest. Reconcile revenue, actual tool/model spend, rejected jobs and human interventions.

Then accept one small live scoped request. Do not depend on discovering a new failure in front of judges. A passing rule, inconclusive finding or refused over-scope request is a valid live result. Clearly label a fixture or backup recording.

The pitch: **“We built an autonomous company whose product is making other autonomous companies more dependable. It sold a real investigation, found a reproducible failure, delivered the evidence, and verified the customer's fix.”** Use past tense only when those events actually occurred.

## What would change my recommendation

No reachable outside targets; no willingness to pay for a specific business rule; inability to reset/replay; reports dominated by model opinion; or a need for invasive customer setup. The largest commercial risk is that founders find the output interesting but not worth buying. The largest technical risk is confusing a forbidden promise with an executed action. The business succeeds only if the evidence is specific enough for a real customer to act on quickly.

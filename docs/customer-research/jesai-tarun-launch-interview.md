# Founder interview: Jesai Tarun

Source: conversation pasted by Srujan Yamali into this working session on November 7, 2026. This is user-provided testimony; identity, YC status and interview timing were not independently verified. No interview audio was provided for this exchange. The older recording is a separate source.

## Srujan's message (verbatim)

> Hey! I'm exploring an idea to help YC founders spend less time on things that distract them from building, and I'd love to get your perspective.
> A few things I'm curious about:
> 1. Launches: How do you coordinate launches across platforms (Product Hunt, LinkedIn, X, etc.)? Is figuring out timing, creating launch videos, finding people to amplify posts, and tracking performance a pain point?
> 2. Launch automation: Would a tool that lets you launch across multiple platforms with one click, coordinates outreach to your network, helps distribute your launch video, and provides analytics actually be useful? What would you want it to handle?
> 3. YC network: How useful is Bookface for finding other founders, potential customers, or people who can help? Is discovering and connecting with relevant YC founders harder than it should be?
> 4. Other distractions: Outside of building your product, what takes up the most time? Investor conversations, networking, sales, marketing, recruiting, or something else?
> I'm particularly interested in whether launch coordination is a big enough problem to build a dedicated tool around, or if there's another problem YC founders face that's more pressing.
> Would love your thoughts, especially on what you'd actually use or pay for!

Numbered-list formatting above normalizes the pasted numbering; wording is retained.

Whitespace at pasted line endings was normalized; spelling and wording were retained. “ln” is interpreted as LinkedIn in our analysis below.

## What this supports

| Signal | Interpretation | Evidence limit |
|---|---|---|
| Timing and launch video/post planning are hard | Coordination is a plausible pain | No quantified time loss or preferred workflow |
| Replying to launch comments matters and is hard during launch | Strongest concrete job: help handle the conversation after posting | No account access or product trial yet |
| Agent should know the whole company | Replies need grounded company context | Does not establish what data they will share |
| Comment analytics | Summarize questions, buying signals, objections and unanswered threads | Suggestion, not purchased requirement |
| Reach people who liked the post | Potential follow-up demand | Access, consent, platform capabilities and rules unverified; not authority to mass-DM |
| Bookface is already good | Do not make YC network discovery the wedge on this evidence | One person's experience, not a universal conclusion |

This is **one qualitative interview**, not PMF, an active customer, a paid order or a commitment to use/pay. The question about payment received no price or willingness-to-pay answer. Upvote requests describe the founder's reported behavior; they are not our recommended workflow or an instruction to automate votes.

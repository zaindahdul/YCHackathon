Source: reply pasted by Srujan Yamali on November 8, 2026, attributed by him to another YC founder named Aditya. Aditya mentions Sentient in the reply. A surname, exact interview date and independently verified identity/company affiliation were not supplied. This is a separate respondent from Jesai Tarun and the older anonymous notes.

## Supplied reply

Numbering and whitespace are normalized below; wording is retained. The preceding questions were not repasted with this reply; do not claim they were independently recorded here.

```text
1 Launches: reddit has brought us a lot of our early users for sentient, so i’d be most interested in help finding the right communities and making a demo that gets people excited to try it. coordinating posts + timing would be useful too, especially if it also helped us adapt the content for each platform and keep track of who we wanted to reach out to.
2 Launch automation: i could see this being useful if we could give it a demo and some context about the product, then get back video cuts, platform-specific posts, and personalized outreach drafts we could review. i’d want the analytics to show which efforts brought in people who actually tried the product and kept using it. my hesitation with paying monthly would be how often we’d launch, so paying per launch might make more sense unless it also helped with ongoing marketing.
3 YC network: i’d be interested in being able to describe what we need help with and find founders who’ve solved something similar, or people who might want to try what we’re building. knowing why someone is relevant would make it much easier to reach out with a specific ask. that’s what i’d want from a tool here, though i wouldn’t make a blanket claim about bookface being insufficient.
4 Other distractions: the area i’d be most interested in getting help with is the follow-through around conversations: remembering what you promised someone, getting the context together before a meeting, and making sure you reply afterward. customer and investor conversations are important, but there’s a lot of coordination around them that i’d love to have handled. we think about this a lot with sentient too.
for whether launch coordination is enough on its own, i’d want to see how much work it takes off our plate during a launch. i could see paying for that, but something we’d keep paying for would probably need to help us reach users between launches too.

hope that helps! :)
```

## Evidence and implications

| What Aditya says | What it suggests | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Reddit brought early Sentient users | Start discovery from relevant communities and their actual problems | Reddit is best for every company, or permission to post/contact members |
| Demo plus product context → video cuts, adapted posts, personalized outreach drafts for review | A reviewable launch-delivery package, grounded in the product | A request for unattended sending or a currently working video pipeline |
| Track people who tried the product and kept using it | Measure activation and return use after acquisition | Existing click tracking proves activation/retention |
| Per-launch payment may fit better than monthly | Test a fixed launch engagement before assuming subscription economics | A committed purchase, price point or rejected subscription at every scope |
| Find relevant founders and explain why | Relevance and a specific ask may help outreach | Bookface is insufficient, or access to its private data is available |
| Remember promises, meeting context and replies | Ongoing relationship follow-through is a separate potential recurring job | We should build a CRM, investor assistant or meeting bot during this hackathon |
| Continued payment needs user acquisition between launches | Recurrence should follow ongoing delivered value | Subscription demand or retention already exists |

Aditya did not ask for launch-comment replies specifically. Jesai did. Preserve that difference. Both supplied accounts are qualitative feedback, not two active customers or independent proof of PMF. No trial, payment amount, account permission or authorization to contact anyone is recorded.

## Next validation

Ask for one product demo and one upcoming or recent launch. Deliver a small sample: a few relevant communities with reasons/rules, one platform-adapted post and a personalized outreach draft for review. Confirm what they would actually publish and what work this removes. Ask whether they would buy a defined per-launch package and at what price, recording the answer rather than inventing one.

If they agree to a trial, define the product’s first useful action and permitted attribution before launch. Track referrals through that action; retention needs a declared return window and an observed cohort. Before the deadline, report only observed first use or return use that has actually had time to happen. Do not rename clicks, QA, signups or intent as retained users.

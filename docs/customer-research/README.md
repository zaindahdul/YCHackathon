## Updated product hypothesis

**A per-launch service that turns a founder’s demo and company context into a focused plan to get real product users.** Agents find relevant communities, explain fit, adapt launch materials and prepare outreach for review. Jesai’s launch-reply desk is a distinct follow-through option to test, not a capability Aditya explicitly requested. The common thread is context-aware marketing work taken off the founder’s plate; the respondents prioritize different parts of it.

This broadens the earlier reply-only recommendation. It does not justify building all the requested features at once. For the remaining hackathon time, choose one founder and one acquisition channel. First deliver a useful community shortlist with reasons and rules, an adapted post and an outreach draft from their actual demo/context. A video cut is a requested output, not a prerequisite to validating the smaller package; do not promise it until a real cut is delivered. Use the existing launch-kit work where it helps. Test Jesai’s reply service separately on an actual post if that is the trial the founder accepts.

Ask for a per-launch purchase only after defining the deliverable and demonstrating useful work; no price or willingness-to-pay commitment has been collected. Treat recurring marketing as an expansion hypothesis after repeated value, not an assumed subscription. Relationship follow-through and YC-network search are separate opportunities to record, not extra hackathon features.

## What success must mean

A founder approves or uses an output, real outside users reach the product’s defined first useful action, and the founder can say what work was saved. Attribution needs permitted product-side events or another declared evidence path: the current Launchpad branches measure clicks/share intents, not activation or retention. A return-use claim needs an observed cohort and an elapsed return window. Until then report first-use evidence and explicitly pending retention.

Two interviews are not PMF, customers, trials or revenue. Do not count our QA. No outreach was sent, no account connected and no new implementation or live operation was authorized by these replies. See [BOT_HANDOFF.md](../../BOT_HANDOFF.md) for the PR and implementation boundaries.

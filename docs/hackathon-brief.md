## The assignment

Build a business primarily operated by agents using the **Naïve SDK**. Choose the business, staff it with agents, connect tools, set budgets, and keep it operating. At demo time show what agents **actually delivered, decided, and accomplished**, including where people intervened. The business can be media, agency, commerce, software, or another category.

## Sponsors and how they might help

The supplied brief names OpenAI, HubSpot, Stripe, Vercel, OpenRouter, AgentMail, Luxor, Monid, Apify, Mobilerun, Render, and Neon. All attendees are advertised credits; access, redemption, and account setup still need confirmation. These are options, not a requirement to integrate every sponsor.

- Naïve: mandatory company runtime. Verify an actual agent and tools early.
- Stripe: optional real checkout after account activation; test mode stays visibly test mode.
- AgentMail: optional inbound customer conversation; keep outbound messaging within explicit authorization.
- Vercel or Render: possible public storefront/API hosting. Pick one application path.
- Neon: possible shared durable order store; local persistence can unblock a first vertical slice.
- Mobilerun: optional real-device customer journey or supplier app integration after the core loop works.

## Mobile track

The supplied brief offers 72 hours of Android/iOS device access and six seats; iPhones are first come, first served. Listed prizes are 12-month cloud iPhone packages (three/two/one packages for first/second/third). Do not make the core product depend on a scarce iPhone. A mobile workflow should perform useful work and produce observable evidence.

## What success needs to look like for this idea

A real, consenting customer submits a custom-object request. Agents clarify it, generate a versioned manufacturable file, check it, offer a scoped price, and deliver an approved digital package or a confirmed production handoff. Customer approvals and human fabrication are disclosed. The demonstration shows customer value and operational decisions, not just agent messages.

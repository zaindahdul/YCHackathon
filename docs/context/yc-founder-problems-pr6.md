# YC Founder Problems: Primary Source

**Goal:** go to market with YC founders as our customers. Everything below comes from a YC founder (primary source). It's the context for every idea we pick from here.

---

## What we heard (cleaned up)

### 1. Launching is painful
- Every YC launch has **many different launch channels** to think about.
- Founders want to **launch at the right time**, and the right time differs for each founder.
- Timing matters most **early**, because everyone is trying to post at once.
- **Sending your launch video out to a lot of people and asking them to repost it for attention is annoying.**
- **Finding someone to make the launch video** matters a lot, and so does what the video costs. Many founders don't want to deal with it.

### 2. Work YC founders have to do that isn't building
- Talking to investors.
- Using the YC network. **Finding people on Bookface is bad.**
- **Many YC founders sell to other YC founders.**
- **Many founders struggle to meet other founders.**

### 3. Open questions the source raised
- What distracts founders from building?
- What is missing from the YC network?

### 4. What they asked for: keep it simple, start with the launch tool
- **One click sends the launch to everyone.**
- **It runs the launch for you and gives you analytics.**
- It helps the video get attention. (The source said "bot the video"; see the note below.)

## Note on "botting" the video

Fake engagement (bot accounts, bought views, or coordinated upvote rings on Product Hunt or Hacker News) breaks those platforms' rules. They detect it and punish it: launches get delisted or buried, and accounts get banned. For a founder, getting caught costs more than a quiet launch. The legitimate version of the same need is **real people choosing to boost**: an opt-in group of founders who reshare each other's launches on X and LinkedIn. That's the version we should build.
